## 1. Why 3.C is being rescoped

As originally posed — *"how much claim traffic can be made declarative?"* — the
question is **unfalsifiable**: anything can be *relabelled* a declaration.
*"I hereby declare this retrieval performed"* is felicitous and useless.

The constraint that makes it testable: **a declarative requires conventional
authority.** A signature is felicitous because the key holder is *entitled* to
sign. The external corpus measured **0.000 declarative** because a bug reporter
has no standing to constitute anything about someone else's software.

**So the route tests convertibility against STANDING, not against imagination.**

## 2. The authority test, verbatim and mechanical

> For claim **C**, is there a party **P** such that
> **(i)** P is inside the system,
> **(ii)** P's saying-so is what **MAKES** C true rather than **reporting** that
> C is true, and
> **(iii)** P's entitlement to say so is established by something the system
> holds — a key, a role, a prior declaration?

| outcome | condition |
|---|---|
| **CONVERTIBLE** | yes on all three |
| **NOT-CONVERTIBLE-(ii)** | the claim **reports** rather than constitutes → stays assertive |
| **NOT-CONVERTIBLE-(iii)** | **no one inside is entitled** → record where the entitlement would have to come from |

### 2a. The rule-3d trap, applied unchanged

A claim can be made trivially "declarative" by declaring something **weaker than
what was wanted**. **For every CONVERTIBLE claim I must state WHAT IS LOST**
relative to the assertive form. **If the declarative form no longer answers the
question the caller asked, it classifies NOT CONVERTIBLE — abandonment, not
conversion.**

## 4. Decision rule

| **3C-CLOSED-NEGATIVE** | convertible assertives ≤ 1 of Gyza's 7, **and** external ≈ 0 | the declarative share is a **property of what a system constitutes**, not a lever. 3.C closes |
| **3C-OPEN-POSITIVE** | ≥ 3 of 7 convertible **and** at least one passes both D2 gates | a real lever remains; something is built |
| **3C-INCONCLUSIVE** | between, or the Part A retrodiction fails | report the number, claim neither |

**A NEGATIVE IS A SUCCESS.** It closes the last untried high-value item and
converts a proposed technique into a scoping result.

**B3 falsifier, stated in advance:** the external corpus should be **near zero
convertible**. **If it comes back materially above zero, the authority test is
too permissive** — and that is a finding about the test, not about the corpus.

**B4:** if Gyza's convertible fraction is very high, ask whether the test
measures convertibility or merely that Gyza wrote its own vocabulary.
